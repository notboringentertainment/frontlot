## Story

Esme Vale sorts letters that wash ashore. She has a blunt dark bob and a coat with a collar turned up against the salt wind.

At low tide, she carries the undelivered letters to Orin Bell. He knows every boat that has gone missing.

## World

The Tide Office is a clapboard room on stilts over a tidal inlet. A single amber lamp burns behind a salt-clouded window.

The Bell Steps lead down to the water. Nobody uses them after dark.
