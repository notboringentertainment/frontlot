# The trailer test

Ben decided, “trim for the trailer test”. This signed definition replaces the
full film's whole-stage requirements for this test. Its five reviews are Story,
Paper cut, Rough cut, Colour and sound, and Final file. Use their existing board
controls and signed gates. A save, technical pass, bare approval flag or terminal
instruction cannot supply a writer's signature.

The dropped whole-stage reviews are the treatment with three options, complete
cast looks, cast portraits, complete visual references with an approved poster,
separate lines and title cards, separate shot list, and the whole footage and
sound collection. Do not ask for them or build their screens for this test.
They remain recorded in the older signed definition for a later deliberate
restoration. Never rewrite that definition or silently migrate a production.

Keep the individual protections for every look and reference actually used,
every paid request, every kept take and each exact card's lettering. One signed
purchase buys one call. Unknown charges remain reserved and block paid work.
Use only Front Lot's governed route. Never retry automatically, substitute a
provider or expose credentials. Use Remotion for local composition and FFmpeg
for finishing; do not offer a different runtime for this signed trailer.

The paper review covers the beats, exact spoken lines and cards, shot list,
timing and story warnings together. Rough-cut approval binds the exact saved
edit and current kept files. Colour and sound has its own signature of that
saved version; the rough-cut signature cannot stand in for it. Any change needs
a fresh review. A later edit or export never erases an earlier version.

The writer watches and listens to the actual final file and signs its exact
bytes, edit revision, measurements and story comparison. Every protected line
must still be heard verbatim. Text on a card, subtitles, a saved script or a
sound stream merely existing is not evidence that it was heard. The screen
requires the writer's actual confirmations; do not invent them. Technical sound
checks measure sound, not understanding. Finish no stage without its verified
signature and matching current material. Upload nothing.

## For the tools

The saved edit's `render_runtime` must be `remotion`. The
`render_runtime_selection` is fixed by this signed trailer contract, not an
agent preference. The alternative `hyperframes` (HyperFrames) is outside this
contract: do not present both as choices or substitute either another renderer
or paid poster generation. Explain the fixed local Remotion and FFmpeg route.
The Paper cut stage is the planning review; there is no treatment or proposal
stage to add back merely to record this fixed runtime.
