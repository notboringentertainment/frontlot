# Sound effects in a trailer

Open Sound effects workspace beside the voice and music controls. Save a description
for one moment in the checked paper cut, choose its length and decide whether it
should loop smoothly. The start belongs to a shot, and the sound must fit inside
the sequence. The brief records intended placement; it does not mix the sound into
a playable cut yet.

Sound Effects v3 receives exactly the description, model, length and loop choice
shown on the spending card. The paper-cut moment stays here. Sign one card to buy
one call. Prices come from the current signed, unexpired account figures and the
same ceiling as voice, music, footage and other paid work. There is no default
sound-effect price and no automatic duration, retry or substitute service. A
missing or expired ceiling stops the request before contact.

Listen to a take, then sign keeping or declining it. Decline is final. Another
take requires its own request and signature. A changed description, moment or file
needs a fresh look; it cannot silently replace what you kept. Start a separate
sound effect to preserve the brief and takes of a different effect.

Bring your own effect uses the Mac file chooser. Save its moment and length first,
then choose a file and write where it came from and its licence note. Its measured
length must match the brief within one frame. Importing costs nothing and sends
nothing to a service. The take still needs your signed decision. Links and path
tricks are refused. A file matching audio this production generated cannot be
relabelled as imported. This cannot identify a file altered or re-encoded elsewhere,
or audio made outside Front Lot entirely. The licence note is your own statement
about its source and permission.

If a response, charge or download is uncertain, its money stays held. All paid
work stops until you check the account and sign a charged or not-charged outcome
in the shared audio controls. Time passing never resolves it. A successful charge
is settled once against the receipt; recorded cash and replacement-value credits
remain separate without counting credits twice.

The account key stays on the server. A missing key is shown as disconnected; this
screen cannot connect an account. A configured key is not proof of account access,
permission or commercial rights. This piece does not clear sound-effect rights,
make a rough cut, place repeated loops, mix levels or finish a production stage.

## For the tools

The tool is elevenlabs_sfx, discovered by the existing registry, with model
eleven_text_to_sound_v3 and POST /v1/sound-generation. The request consumes only a
registered project_dir and saved audio_request_id; any output_path must match its
canonical take location. Half a second to thirty seconds and whole frames at
twenty-four frames per second are required. The conservative minimum follows the
API reference even though the overview describes a shorter range.

The additive signed setting is audio_price_basis.effect_credits_per_second, with
value, set_at and expires_at. The quote rounds credits upward, multiplies by the
signed dollars_per_credit, then rounds dollars upward to four decimal places.
No price from a public page is built into the code. The pinned trailer definition
is left byte for byte unchanged: its existing signature remains valid. The board's
restricted effects routes extend the existing cue adapter, chooser, signer and
shared spending snapshot; no unrestricted assistant operation was added.

Public provider documentation checked on November 13, 2026 UTC:
[call contract](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert)
and [effect capabilities and pricing unit](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/sound-effects).
The overview reports forty credits per specified second and recommends the newer
model as the previous model approaches retirement. This is dated public evidence,
not a quote for the owner's account. No account endpoint was queried.
